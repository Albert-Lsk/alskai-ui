01

Two drums, three colours.两个滚筒，三种颜色。

A stencil duplicator lays down one ink per drum. Where pink lands on blue, the inks multiply into a deep violet that neither drum carries. Specimen separates your figures into two drums the same way a print shop would, so a plate scanned in 1904 comes out like it was pulled this morning.孔版印刷机一个滚筒只上一种墨。粉色压在蓝色上，两种墨叠成一种深紫，这个颜色哪个滚筒里都没有。标本会像印刷店师傅那样，把你的图拆成两版。1904 年扫描的图版印出来，像今天早上刚印的。
