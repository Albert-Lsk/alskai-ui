02 / Rhythm节奏

Every poster has a tempo.每张海报，都有自己的拍子。

Concentric arcs, each turning at a different subdivision of the beat. Scroll, move the cursor, or press play.一圈圈圆弧，各按不同的分拍转动。滚动、移动鼠标，或者直接按播放。

03 / Rules原则

Five rules Raster will not break.栅格不会破的五条规矩。

Borrowed from the International Typographic Style, and written into the engine.从国际主义平面设计借来，直接写进了引擎。

01

The grid comes first.网格先于内容。

You pick columns, margins and a baseline before a single word goes in. Everything else is placed, never pushed.一个字还没放，先定栏、边距和基线。之后的东西都是摆上去的，不是挤进去的。

02

Flush left, ragged right.左边对齐，右边自然参差。

No forced justification, no rivers of white. Raster watches the rag and fixes the lines that leave holes.不硬拉两端对齐，也不留一条条白河。栅格盯着右边的参差，专修那些留下大洞的行。

03

One typeface, few sizes.一种字体，几个字号。

A modular scale with six steps. If you need a seventh, you probably need fewer words.一套六级的字号阶梯。要是还想加第七级，多半是字太多了。

04

Asymmetry is balance.不对称也是平衡。

Weight on one side, air on the other. Centred layouts are allowed, but Raster will ask you why.一边放分量，一边留空气。居中也可以，但栅格会问你一句：为什么？

05

Leave the white alone.留白不用填。

Empty columns are part of the design. Raster never fills them with stock photos to look busy.空着的栏也是设计的一部分。栅格不会为了显得热闹，往里面塞图库照片。

04 / Engine引擎

Three things it does all day.它整天在做的三件事。

Live grids活的网格

Change the column count on a 40-page report and every spread reflows in place. Hover the drawing to watch.一份 40 页的报告改一次栏数，每一页都会原地重排。把鼠标放到图上看看。

Every line of text in Raster lands on the same 24-point baseline, across columns, captions and footnotes, so facing pages read as one block.栅格里的每一行字，都落在同一条 24 点基线上。正文、图注、脚注跨栏对齐，左右两页读起来像一整块。

Baseline lock基线锁定

The red rules are the baseline. Captions and body text share it, even at different sizes.红线就是基线。图注和正文字号不同，也踩在同一套线上。

Rag control参差控制

Short lines that leave holes and lonely last words are marked in red, with a fix suggested before print.留下大洞的短行、孤零零的末行单字，都会被标成红色，付印前给出改法。
