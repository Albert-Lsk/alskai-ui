Four passes, one window四道工序，一扇窗

Every window is built in the same order.花窗向来按这个顺序装。

Keep scrolling and the rose goes up the way glaziers have done it for eight hundred years. Vitrail builds your palette the same way.继续往下滚，这扇玫瑰窗会按八百年来玻璃匠的做法一步步装起来。Vitrail 搭配色，也是这个顺序。

  1. Stone tracery先砌石框The frame comes first. In Vitrail that's your grid and spacing.框架最先定，在 Vitrail 里就是你的栅格和间距。
  2. Lead cames再走铅条Lead divides one colour from the next: your borders and dividers.铅条把颜色和颜色隔开，对应边框和分割线。
  3. Glass镶上玻璃Each pane takes a colour from your palette, in proportion.每块玻璃从色板里取一个颜色，多少按比例来。
  4. Light最后放光Then the sun. A palette only counts once light has gone through it.最后是阳光。光透过去了，这套配色才算数。
