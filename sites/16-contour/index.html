Every ridge has a saddle.每一道山脊，

Saddle draws mountains the way surveyors did, one line for every 20 metres of height. Drag your route across and see how hard the day will be before you leave the car.鞍部用测绘员的办法画山：每升高 20 米，就画一条线。把路线拖过去，还没下车，就知道这一天会有多累。

02 · Reading the lines02 · 读图

Learn four shapes and you can read any mountain.认得这四种形状，
就读得懂任何一座山。

A contour joins every point at the same height. How close the lines sit and which way they bend tell you the rest. Under each map is the slice you would walk.等高线就是把一样高的点连起来。线挨得多近、往哪边弯，山的样子就全在里面了。每张图下面，是沿中线走一遍的横剖面。

Closed rings that shrink inward. The smallest ring is the top.一圈套一圈，越往里越小，最小那圈就是山顶。

Two sets of rings back to back. The low point between them is where you cross.两组圈背靠背，中间那个最低的地方，就是翻山的路口。

Valley山谷V
