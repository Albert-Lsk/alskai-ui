Workflows that grow on their own.种下一条流程，

Tend strings approvals, invoices and reminders into one living workflow. Plant it once and it keeps itself watered: retries, waits, reroutes.Tend 把审批、对账、催款提醒这些重复的活儿连成一条线。种下去之后，重试、等待、改道它都自己来，你不用天天去浇水。

From seed to harvest从播种到收获

You plant it once. Tend does the gardening.你只种一次，
剩下的园艺交给 Tend。

Most automations wilt the first time an API times out. Tend expects bad weather, so every step knows how to wait, retry and find another way round.大多数自动化流程，接口超时一次就蔫了。Tend 默认天气会变坏，所以每一步都会等、会重试，也会绕路。

01

Plant a trigger播种：选一个起点

An email, a form, a new row in a sheet. Anything that happens can start a run.一封邮件、一张表单、表格里新加的一行，发生的任何事都能当起点。

Form submitted有人提交了表单
Email to finance@财务邮箱收到邮件
New row in “Orders”“订单”表新增一行
Every Monday 9:00每周一早上 9 点
02

Let it tend itself照料：它自己管自己

Failures retry with back-off, slow steps wait politely, and dead ends reroute.失败了就隔一会儿再试，慢的步骤就耐心等，走不通就换一条路。

03

Harvest the hours收获：省下来的时间

Every run reports what it saved, so you can see the field getting greener.每次运行都会记下省了多少时间，你能看着这片地一点点变绿。
