Vorkurs · a six-week foundation course预科 · 六周设计基础课

Vorkurs teaches form, colour and material the way the Bauhaus did in 1919: paper, scissors, wood, and a lot of play. Every shape on this page is solid enough to pick up. Go on, throw one.预科教形状、颜色和材料，用的是包豪斯 1919 年那套办法：纸、剪刀、木头，再加上大量的玩。这页上的每个形状都拿得起来，不信扔一个试试。

01 · Fragebogen, Weimar 192301 · 1923 年魏玛的一张问卷

three shapes. three colours. one right answer?三个形状，三种颜色，
有标准答案吗？

In 1923 Wassily Kandinsky gave everyone at the Bauhaus a questionnaire: fill the triangle, the square and the circle with yellow, red or blue. He was sure the question had a correct answer. Pick a colour, then click a shape.1923 年，康定斯基给包豪斯的每个人发了一张问卷：给三角形、正方形和圆各填一种颜色，黄、红或蓝。他相信这道题有标准答案。先选一个颜色，再点一个形状。
