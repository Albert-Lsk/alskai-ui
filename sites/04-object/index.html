How a payment moves一笔钱怎么走

Three hands touch every dollar. The last one is yours.每一笔钱都经过三道手，最后一道是你。

01

Drafted发起

Anyone on the team can draft a payment, attach the invoice and pick the account.团队里谁都能发起付款，附上发票，选好从哪个账户出。

02

Checked复核

Rules look at limits, payees and timing, and flag anything that doesn't look like you.规则会核对额度、收款方和时间，不像你们平时会做的，就先拦下来。

03

Signed签名

It only leaves when a key holder presses down. The key never touches the internet.只有持钥人按下去，钱才会走。密钥本身从不联网。
