The process流程

Coat, expose, rinse, keep.涂布，曝光，水洗，
然后留着。

  1. Coat · Collect涂布 · 收集One shortcut saves anything: a page, a frame of video, a photo from your phone.一个快捷键，什么都能存：网页、视频里的一帧、手机里的照片。
  2. Expose · Press曝光 · 压平Each clip is flattened into a still print, so a page that changes tomorrow stays as you saw it.每一条都压成一张静止的图。网页明天改了，你存下的还是那天看到的样子。
  3. Rinse · Clean水洗 · 去杂Ads, cookie banners and tracking links wash away. Only the thing you wanted stays white.广告、弹窗、跟踪链接全部洗掉，只留下你想要的那部分。
  4. Oxidise · Keep氧化 · 沉淀A cyanotype keeps darkening for a day after the wash. Your archive does too: the more you use it, the richer the labels get.蓝晒洗完以后，还会再变深一整天。图库也一样，用得越多，标签越准。

An archive that reads like a herbarium像标本册一样的图库

Anna Atkins pressed every specimen flat, printed it and wrote its name on a slip. Pressed does the same for the things you save: one print, one label, nothing else on the page.安娜·阿特金斯把每一株海藻压平、晒成图，再贴一张写着名字的小纸条。Pressed 对你存下的东西也这么做：一张图，一个标签，别的都不要。
