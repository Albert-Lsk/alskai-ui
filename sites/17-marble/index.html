The five strokes五道工序

A pattern is only five strokes deep.一张纹样，
只要走五道。

The same bath, photographed after each stroke. Colours float on a thickened size; every tool only pushes them sideways, never mixes them.同一盆水，每走一道拍一张。颜料浮在调稠的胶液上，每样工具都只是把颜色往旁边推，从来不会把它们搅混。

Drop滴墨

brush & ox gall毛刷和牛胆汁

Colours are flicked onto the size. Each new drop shoves the last ones aside into veins.颜料一滴滴弹到胶液上，后一滴把前面的挤成纹路。

a single stylus一根针

One pin is drawn down the bath and back up, a hand's width at a time.一根针从上划到下，再从下划回来，一掌宽走一趟。

One craft, three names一门手艺，三个名字

The same water, told three ways.同一盆水，
三种叫法。

Marbling turns up wherever paper and patience met. We borrowed one of its names for our own.有纸、又有耐心的地方，大多有过这门手艺。我们借了其中一个名字。

Ebru

Turkish土耳其

The Turkish name, often traced to a Persian word for clouds.土耳其的叫法，常被说成来自波斯语里的“云”。

Suminagashi

Japanese日本

The Japanese name. It means, quite plainly, ink set flowing.日本的叫法，写成汉字是“墨流”，意思就是让墨流动。

流沙笺

Chinese中国

The Chinese name: flowing-sand letter paper. Look at the lines and it fits.中国的叫法。名字里有流沙，纸上的纹路看着也真像流沙。
