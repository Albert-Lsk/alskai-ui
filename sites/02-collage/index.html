QWhy does the earring in Vermeer's painting look so bright?维米尔画里那颗耳环，为什么看起来那么亮？
Because very little of it is actually painted. The pearl is a few strokes: one sharp white highlight on top 1 and a soft reflection of her white collar below 2. Your eye fills in the rest. The turban's blue, ground from costly ultramarine, keeps the cool light consistent across her face 3. 因为它其实没画几笔。整颗珍珠只有寥寥几笔：顶上一点锐利的白色高光 1，底下一抹衣领的柔和反光 2，剩下的由你的眼睛自己补上。头巾用的是昂贵的群青，让整张脸的冷光保持一致 3。
