02 · Security layers02 · 防伪层

Four layers no office printer can copy.办公室打印机，
印不出这四道防线。

Security printers have spent two centuries making paper hard to fake. Tessera renders the same tricks into every PDF and every printout, so a forgery fails long before anyone checks a database.印钞厂花了两百年，让纸难以伪造。契纹把同样的手法印进每一份 PDF 和每一张打印件，伪造品不用查数据库，拿在手上就露馅。

AMicrotext微缩文字

Lines that turn out to be words. Hover the card to read them.看着是线，凑近了是字。把鼠标移到卡片上读一读。

BIris printing彩虹印刷

Colours that melt into each other in one pass. A colour copier has to split them, and the seams show.一次印出无缝过渡的颜色。复印机只能分色重印，接缝一眼就看得出。

CLatent image隐形图案

Tilt the card. A mark made of lines at another angle appears, and a scanner flattens it away.把卡片斜过来，换个角度的线条拼出一个记号。扫描仪一扫，它就没了。

SERIAL · 连续编号

DSerial numbering连续编号

Every document takes the next number, exactly once. Two papers with the same serial means one of them is fake.每份文件按顺序领号，同一个号码只发一次。两张纸号码相同，必有一张是假的。

03 · API03 · 接口

Three lines to issue. One link to verify.签发只要三行，
验证只要一个链接。

Send the document, get back a print-ready PDF with its rosette, border and serial already engraved, plus a public link anyone can check.把文件发过来，拿回一份已经印好花纹、边框和编号的 PDF，再附一个谁都能打开核对的链接。

  • Vector output, sharp at any print size矢量输出，印多大都清楚
  • Verification works offline from the paper itself没有网络，也能对着纸本身核验
  • Your own colours, portrait and microtext颜色、肖像和微缩文字都可以换成你自己的
